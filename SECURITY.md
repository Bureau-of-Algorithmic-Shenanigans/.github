# Security

Found a vulnerability in one of our repositories? Please report it privately, not in a public issue:
open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**
([how it works](https://docs.github.com/code-security/security-advisories/guidance-on-reporting-and-writing-information-about-vulnerabilities/privately-reporting-a-security-vulnerability)).

You'll get an answer within a few days. Repositories with their own `SECURITY.md` may give more specific instructions.
