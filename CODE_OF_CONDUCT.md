# Code of Conduct

Our repositories follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, discuss the work and not the person, and leave out harassment, personal
attacks and discriminatory language.

**Reporting:** tell the maintainers privately through the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**
(write "Code of conduct" in the title). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues
or block participants who violate this code.
